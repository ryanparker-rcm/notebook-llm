Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

What stays on your device

  • The books, PDFs, links and pasted text you add.
  • Your chat history for the current session, your settings, your streak and badges.
  • Your API key, which is stored only on your computer.

What is sent over the internet

  • Your questions. To answer, the app sends your question and the passages of your sources that are relevant to it to an AI service (Google's Gemini API), using your API key. Your whole library is not uploaded.
  • Your account. When you create an account, your email and password are handled by an authentication service (Firebase Authentication, provided by Google). We never see your password.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or show you ads.
  • We do not read your books or chats.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time from Settings.
  • Remove any source with the ✕ button, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in screen to reset your password by email.
